# results_rev6 — in-domain results of REV6 Run 1

This folder holds the contents of `results_rev6_indomain.zip`: `parts/` with the daily observed and forecast indices
of every cell, lead and method (raw, QM and VI) and the fitted post-processing, plus the verification tables
(`warning_day.csv`, `warning_onset.csv`, `warning_episode.csv`, `warning_regional.csv`, `continuous_index_space.csv`,
`metrics_original_scale.csv`, `thr_deltas.csv`, `postproc_params.csv`) and `config_rev6.json`.
`paper_analysis_rev6/` recomputes every table and figure of the article from `parts/`.
